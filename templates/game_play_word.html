📋 How to Play

  • 1 One student thinks: The teacher picks one student who thinks of a word and keeps it secret.
  • 2 Everyone else asks: The rest of the class asks yes/no questions to narrow it down.
  • 3 Answer honestly: The person thinking must answer every question truthfully.
  • 4 Make a guess: When you think you know the word, the teacher confirms if you're right.
  • 5 Keep score: Count how many questions it took to guess—fewer is better.
